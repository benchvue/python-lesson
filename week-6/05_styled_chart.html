Styling a Chart

colors, a zero line and a grid turn a plain chart into a readable report
3months = ["Jan", "Feb", "Mar", "Apr"]
4profit = [8000, -3000, 5000, 12000]
6colors = ["green" if p >= 0 else "red" for p in profit]
9plt.bar(months, profit, color=colors)
10plt.axhline(0, color="black")
12plt.grid(axis="y", linestyle="--")
13plt.savefig("monthly_profit.png")
15plt.show()
