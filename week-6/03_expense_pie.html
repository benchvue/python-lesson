Pie Chart — Show Proportions

plt.pie() splits a whole into slices — great for a spending breakdown
1import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
3labels = ["Rent", "Salary", "Office", "Travel"]
4amounts = [12000, 45000, 2500, 8000]
6plt.pie(amounts, labels=labels, autopct="%1.1f%%")
7plt.title("Expense Breakdown")
8plt.savefig("expense_pie.png")
10plt.show()
