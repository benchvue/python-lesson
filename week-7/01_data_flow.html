The only new idea this week

Boxes 04–06 are last week's lesson: dictionaries, lists, pandas, matplotlib. Boxes 01–03 are new, and every error today will come from those three — no internet, a typo in the URL, a key that does not exist. That is why Step 3 stops at a printed table and does not draw anything.
